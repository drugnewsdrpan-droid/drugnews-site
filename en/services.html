Company-service FAQ

We help companies make value logic clear, not turn analysis into advertising.

The goal is to help professional readers understand clinical evidence, business strategy, and capital-market signals.

Who should use Drugnews company services?

Drugnews company services are designed for public biotech companies, startups, pharma companies, CDMOs, medical-technology companies, and IR teams that need to explain clinical evidence, technology platforms, licensing transactions, or commercialization strategy more clearly.

Is this advertising or editorial analysis?

The goal is not to turn a company deck into advertising. Drugnews uses public information, company-approved disclosure, and research frameworks to help professional readers understand the company's value logic, risks, and catalysts.

Can Drugnews help with English content for global readers?

Yes. Drugnews maintains English articles and English service pages, and can help turn Chinese company stories into clearer English narratives for international investors and industry readers.

How does a company start a collaboration?

A company can submit the collaboration form or email drugnews.dr.pan@gmail.com. Drugnews first clarifies the market question, available disclosure, target readers, and suitable content format.
