Default and rejection

On a first visit, analytics_storage, ad_storage, ad_user_data, and ad_personalization all default to denied. If you refuse or have not chosen, the site does not load gtag.js, send Google Analytics collect requests, or create _ga cookies.

Consent and withdrawal

After consent, only analytics storage becomes granted; all advertising-related choices remain denied. You can withdraw through “Privacy settings” in the footer. If analytics has loaded, withdrawal stores the denied status, asks the Google tag to stop analytics, attempts to delete _ga cookies for this domain, and reloads the page so subsequent browsing does not load analytics code.

Browser controls

You can also clear cookies and site data, block third-party cookies, or restrict JavaScript in your browser. Blocking necessary localStorage does not prevent article reading, but the site may be unable to remember your privacy choice.
