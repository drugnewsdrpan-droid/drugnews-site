## Core Thesis

Biogen's tau bet is important because Alzheimer's drug development is searching for the next credible axis after years of amyloid controversy. BIIB080 can only change the story if biomarker movement translates into clinical slowing that physicians, regulators, and payers can trust.

## Why It Matters

The investment question is not simply whether tau is a famous target. It is whether the study design, endpoints, patient selection, biomarker logic, safety profile, and competitive context can create a product that is meaningfully differentiated from existing Alzheimer's approaches.
