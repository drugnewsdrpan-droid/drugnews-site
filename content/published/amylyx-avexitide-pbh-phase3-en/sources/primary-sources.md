## Fixed Boundaries

- Avexitide is not FDA approved. A year-end 2026 NDA and a potential 2027 launch are forward-looking plans.
- The 55% figure is a reduction in event rate versus placebo, not a 55% cure rate.
- Amylyx's estimate of 160,000 symptomatic U.S. patients is not a confirmed diagnosed or treated population.
- Absolute event rates, confidence intervals and subgroup data remain pending full disclosure.
- Imapextide provided proof of concept for less-frequent dosing, but MBX said it would not fund a Phase 2b PBH trial; it must not be described as an actively advancing competitor.
