CAR-T Enters a New Era: From Blood Cancer to Autoimmune Disease

CAR-T's move from blood cancer into autoimmune disease could redefine cell therapy, but the value depends on whether deep immune reset can justify manufacturing complexity, safety risk, and cost.

Core Thesis

Autoimmune CAR-T is important because it changes the purpose of cell therapy. The goal is no longer only killing cancer cells, but potentially resetting pathogenic immune memory in diseases such as lupus and other antibody-driven disorders.

Why It Matters

Investors should focus on durability of remission, steroid-free outcomes, relapse patterns, safety, manufacturing scalability, and whether the therapy can move from elite centers into a repeatable clinical model.
